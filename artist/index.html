01 / THE STORY

MADE OF
MUSIC

An independent sound

GHUKASI is the performing name of Denis Ghukasyan (Денис Гукасян), a singer and independent artist creating electronic music across Deep House, Tech House, EDM and Dance.

His sound combines deep bass, atmospheric melodies, energetic dance rhythms and memorable vocal hooks. Each track brings together melodic feeling and club energy.

His official catalog includes the DREAMS album and singles such as Fell in Love, Adrenaline, In the Sound and Fly Away. The music is available through official streaming platforms.

EDUCATION / DENIS GHUKASYAN

Beyond the music

Denis Ghukasyan studied graphic design at Dilijan School of Art, graduating with honors, and mathematics and programming at Russian-Armenian University. His education is another side of the person behind GHUKASI.
